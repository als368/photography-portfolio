About me

I'm Alsu, a photographer who loves documenting women in STEM: the panels, the competitions, the quiet moments between sessions. My style is bright, clean and warm, built to make people look as capable and confident as they are.

From networking events to hospital research programs to gallery exhibits, I'm always looking for the frame that tells the whole story.
